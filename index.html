About this Demonstration

This Demonstration illustrates the normal vibrational modes of a drum head fixed along a circular edge. Each mode with elongation (in polar coordinates) ψ(t; r, φ) ∼ cos(ωn,k t) cos(n φ) Jn(jn,k r) is related to Bessel functions of the first kind, Jn(jn,k r), where r is the radial coordinate, φ the polar angle, and t the time. Selecting the order, n, determines the angular periodicity of the drum head. Selecting the kth zero jn,k of Jn(x) determines the number of antinodes along a radial line connecting the center and outer edge.

The time slider sets the phase ωn,kt from 0 to 2π, as in the original. With “play at relative frequency” on, each mode oscillates at a rate proportional to jn,k, so higher modes visibly vibrate faster.
